* Valentina IacovelliPhotographerFotografa

Valentina Iacovelli is a photographer. Disguise and characters, she says, allow her “to be present and absent at the same time”: a way of showing herself without having to expose herself completely.

From costume to photography

Valentina first studied fashion and costume, then went on to art studies at the Academy. During her fashion studies she worked as a stylist and as an assistant costume designer.

Photography came during her years at the Academy. She started by photographing herself, then objects, then other people. Looking back, she calls it “almost an initiatory path”: at first the camera was a way of looking at and knowing herself, then a way of coming closer to others. That wish to know someone, she says, is still one of the main reasons she photographs.

She considers herself self-taught. Her studies helped her build a strong artistic identity, but she believes training gives only part of the tools, and the rest comes from practice, experiment and dedication. Some things, in her view, cannot be taught at all: the search for harmony, an aesthetic sensitivity, a personal way of seeing. She speaks of it as a vocation, something to be cultivated and questioned all the time.

One of the first photographs she felt was truly hers showed the leg of an antique doll reflected in a small mirror, next to a dead butterfly she had found in the street. What moved her was the meeting of the two: an object that imitates a living figure, and a creature that had really lived. Two existences, she says, joined by the same stillness.

“The relationship between what has never been alive and what is no longer.”

How she builds her scenes, and the photographs that came out of them, are in the article ‘Valentina - Photographs’.

Print

Her only book so far is a self-produced artist’s book on self-portraiture, “La culla della strega”. It is handmade, with each photograph pinned to the page “like a small memory kept in an old family album”, and she calls it a sort of reliquary that keeps “all the characters who live in me”.

Today

She has several finished projects she wants to release, and is working on two new ones, one around costume and one very different, which she prefers not to define yet. She would like to publish a book of her whole photographic work and hold a solo exhibition, in a space close to her sensitivity.
