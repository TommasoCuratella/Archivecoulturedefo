* ‘Eclipse’ - Laura EnricoIdeation of a fashion film based on a visual storytelling, 2025

01 / 03

“Eclipse” tells the story of an encounter between good and evil, two opposing forces that fear each other, yet are simultaneously drawn together.

In an atmosphere filled with silence and hope, the two shadows move, aware of one another. They observe each other, follow the same footsteps, and mirror each other’s movements.

Every step is charged with tension and confrontation. As they draw closer, boundaries begin to dissolve: the two shadows reflect one another, merge, and overlap.

Eclipse is the moment when light and shadow brush against each other: a fragile, tense balance, yet one that is inevitably complementary.
